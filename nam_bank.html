N2Z Bank

NAM models for the MS-60B+ · nam2zoom 1.2.3's packaging and trainer

Models

Up to 10 NAM models in one effect. Compact models (those nam2zoom.app has converted, in its folder Converted_NAM/Compact) go in as they are. Any other NAM, a capture from TONE3000 for instance, is trained into a Compact one first, with nam2zoom.app's own trainer: about 7½ minutes each on an M1, one after the other.

From Powered by TONE3000

Pick a capture in TONE3000's own window. Only what you pick is fetched; it keeps its creator and license, and a tone whose license allows no changes is not trained.

MS Plus Bridge ×

The MS Plus Bridge works with TONE3000 to give you access to a massive library of Neural Amp Modeler (NAM) captures and IRs of real analog gear, created by a global community of musicians.

You sign in on TONE3000 with your email address and a 6-digit code; no key of your own is needed.

Before installing

  • The bank replaces the N2Z Bank on the pedal: same file name. Install it in the Installer tab; it asks for a backup first. AUTO SAVE off, and try it in a patch you do not save.
  • Saved patches that use the bank play the new models in the order above; the Model knob picks them. nam2zoom.app refuses to replace its bank while any saved patch holds an effect that is not Zoom's own. Here the saved patches that use the bank are named after the build, and the choice is yours.
  • The installer asks for a category. PREAMP, the bank's own, leaves it as nam2zoom builds it. Any other makes the installer adapt it (another id), and nam2zoom.app then refuses to replace that bank: from then on install N2Z banks only here.
  • The bank is nam2zoom's precompiled one with only the models and names filled in: no code is built. Its DSP reservation is 150 of 270.
  • Training uses nam2zoom.app's trainer, set up there once (PyTorch, about 1 GB). Close nam2zoom.app while training here: both use the same cache. A trained model is kept where nam2zoom.app keeps its own, in Converted_NAM/Compact.
