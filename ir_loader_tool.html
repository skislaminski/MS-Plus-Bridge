IR Loader Tool

Put your own cab IRs into the IRLoader effect for the Zoom MS-60B+: 8 slots. Each IR is fitted to what the effect plays, a 64-tap FIR plus 16 biquads, which costs about a third of the DSP of a long convolution. Drop WAV files on a slot, or on the page to fill the slots from the first free one.

Putting the effect onto the pedal
  1. The new file has the same file name and id as the IRLoader on the pedal. Take IRLoader out of every patch (saved ones too), uninstall it, then install the new IRLOADER.ZD2 + IRLOADER.ZIC with the Zoom MS Installer (or stomphacks' pedal_diy.py).
  2. AUTO SAVE off, a backup of the pedal first. Try the effect in a patch you do not save, quietly at first; save it only after switching the pedal off and on once.
  3. The IR knob picks the slot and shows its name (the slot's name field, up to 7 characters: letters, digits, space, . + -), Mix blends dry bass and cab, Level 80 is unity. The cab is mono: put IRLoader after the amp or drive and before stereo effects.
  4. Untested on the pedal so far (prototype 0.21). It needs 1 KB of delay memory and declares a DSP load of 24 (of 270).
What happens to a WAV
  • Any sample rate and format the browser can read (WAV, AIFF, FLAC …) is resampled to 44.1 kHz; several channels are averaged.
  • Trim: everything before the first sample above 1 % of the peak goes, less 4 samples, so the cab lines up with the dry bass when Mix blends them. Up to 371 ms of the IR is kept.
  • The fit: the IR's magnitude, averaged over 1/6 octave, is matched by a 64-tap FIR (the first 1.45 ms of a cab and its broad shape) and 16 biquads (a high-pass, a low-pass and 14 peaks), the bass and the cab's high edge weighted most. The slot shows both curves and the largest deviation between 40 Hz and 6 kHz; above ±1.5 dB it warns. The same WAV always gives the same cab in the same browser.
  • Match loudness: the cab is scaled so that its mean power gain from 40 Hz to 5 kHz is 0 dB, so every slot plays about as loud as the dry bass.
  • An IRLOADER.ZD2 of version 0.10 (1024-tap IRs) can be opened: its IRs are taken over and fitted; the file you write is version 0.21. Names longer than 7 characters are cut.
Where the method comes from
  • A short FIR plus biquads fitted to the smoothed magnitude: HYBRID IR by Leemuzhko (github.com/Leemuzhko/HYBRID-IR, MIT) and the CabIR effect and cab_loader.js by Roman Rusinov (github.com/themanro/ZoomMultistompZDL, MIT), both for the older MultiStomp pedals. The code here is written anew.
  • The biquads run as state-variable filters after Andrew Simper (Cytomic, "Linear Trapezoidal Integrated SVF"): in single precision they stay far closer to the exact filter at bass frequencies than the usual biquad form.
  • The effect itself is built with stomphacks by Thomas Hammer (MIT).
