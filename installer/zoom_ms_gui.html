Zoom MS Installer

Do not switch the pedal off. Do not unplug power or USB. The effect list on the pedal is in doubt – a transfer could not be verified, or the list is not valid. Leave the pedal powered and connected and run the rescue; restart the pedal only after the effect list is reported valid.

Connect the pedal by USB, switch it on and close other programs that use it (nam2zoom, Handy Guitar Lab, browser tabs with a pedal editor). Then press Connect.
